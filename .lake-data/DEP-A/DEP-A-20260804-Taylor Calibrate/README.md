Its central technical focus is: We propose Taylor-Calibrate , a lightweight two-stage initialization method for GDN-based hybrid distillation. We formulate hybrid linear-attention conversion as an initialization problem for recurrent dynamics, not just a projection-copying problem. Hybrid linear attention models offer an appealing path to faster long-context inference: they reduce the quadratic cost and KV-cache burden of full softmax attention while retaining much of the quality of Transformer models.
