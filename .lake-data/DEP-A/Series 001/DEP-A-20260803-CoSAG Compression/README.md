Its central technical focus is: Our CoSAG (d) is training-free: it binds Gaussians to spatially grounded anchors and entropy-codes the resulting binding, reaching sub-megabyte storage with no decoder and higher accuracy. We realize this paradigm as CoSAG (Compact Semantic Anchor Gaussians). We propose CoSAG, a fully training-free framework that builds the field through a closed-form lift over spatially grounded anchors and stores it with a spatially predictive entropy coder, retaining no decoder.
