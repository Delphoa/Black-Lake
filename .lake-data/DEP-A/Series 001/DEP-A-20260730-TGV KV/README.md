Its central technical focus is: Based on these key findings, we propose a robust and T ext- G rounded KV eviction framework for V LMs, i.e., TGV 1 1 1 TGV is also the abbreviation of Train à Grande Vitesse, the France high-speed railway system. Our main contributions are summarized as follows: We take a systematic study of the attention pattern in VLMs and propose TGV-KV, an out-of-the-box KV eviction approach for multimodal KV eviction. Notably, with an extreme retention rate of 5%, TGV-KV reduces the memory consumption of KV cache size from 3.91 GB to a mere 0.20 GB under an 8k context.
