Its central technical focus is: Relying on the controlling strategies of existing image editing methods [ 20 , 21 ] to preserve the visual appearance or spatial structure of the source video in generative frames, many zero-shot video editing methods focus mainly on how to maintain temporal consistency throughout the edited video. In this work, we propose a novel zero-shot video editing framework (based on T2I diffusion models) that enables preserving the temporal structure of the source video across generated frames, i.e., Temporal Structure Preservation Video Editing (TSPVE). To reconcile latent token merging efficiency with source video temporal structure preservation in the denoising process of zero-shot video editing, and mitigate the inflexibility of traditional token merging with fixed parameters for video semantic dynamics, we propose a clip-adaptive token merging strategy based on AVP-AFS outputs.

## Insights and Relevance

For Black Lake, the most useful downstream implication is: Treat zero-shot video editing as trajectory-preserving transformation: retain clip boundaries, anchor frames, merge ancestry, and source-to-output temporal alignment, then reject edits whose narrative-event order or motion structure falls outside validated tolerances.
