### `2606.00734-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: EMA attaches compact Markers to HNSW graph edges. Markers summarize attributes of reachable or dominated nodes, a Codebook discretizes mixed numerical and categorical predicates, joint search consults both geometry and predicates, and bounded edge recovery protects navigability at low selectivity.
