## Summary of Items

### `README.md`

Defines the archival boundary and gives canonical public provenance without exposing private machine context.

### `2606.22924-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: MythraGen embeds WikiArt images with BLIP-2, retrieves examples close to the prompt, then fine-tunes two LoRA adapters that separate artist/style and genre/style signals before combining them with Stable Diffusion for generation.

## Insights and Relevance
