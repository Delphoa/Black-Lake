### `2606.29580-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: MAM-AI is an offline-first Android medical assistant combining a roughly 300M-parameter embedder with a 4B int4 generator. It packages clinical documents, retrieval, and response policies on device so basic access can continue without a cloud connection.
