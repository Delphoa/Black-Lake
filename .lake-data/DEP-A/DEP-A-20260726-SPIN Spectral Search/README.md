### `2606.21535-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: Spectral Search represents a retrieval corpus as a graph and applies SPIN graph wiring so semantically related documents exchange structure before ranking. It combines cosine similarity with a Rayleigh-quotient-style spectral energy intended to surface items that are relevant both locally and globally.

## Insights and Relevance

For Black Lake, the most useful downstream implication is: Learn a query-conditioned spectral mixing weight with a conservative vector-only fallback, and publish topology diagnostics so retrieval gains can be separated from accidental graph density effects.
