### `2607.11783-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: Because such models may hallucinate providing incorrect answers for queries that require up-to-date or domain-specific knowledge, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has been introduced as a solution to connect LLMs with external knowledge sources huang2025survey ; farquhar2024detecting . Despite its risks, limited attention has been paid to how RAG frameworks shape ideological discourse and how generation parameters can influence LLM outputs in such contexts. While prior work shows that changes in temperature affect output diversity, creativity, coherence, and reasoning quality peeperkorn2024temperature ; renze2024effect ; li2025exploring , limited attention has been given to its influence on LLMs’ responses based on external knowledge conveying ideological texts.

## Insights and Relevance

For Black Lake, the most useful downstream implication is: Separate retrieved-source ideology from sampling-induced expression by logging source stance, prompt, temperature, model revision, and response features under one experiment identity, then test whether intervention effects survive topic and corpus shifts.
