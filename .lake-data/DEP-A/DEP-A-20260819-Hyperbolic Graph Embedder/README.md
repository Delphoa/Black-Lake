Its central technical focus is: In this work, we use both synthetic and empirical networks to evaluate hyperbolic embedding methods on two tasks: link prediction and topology reconstruction. Our main contributions are: a benchmark of 13 representative hyperbolic embedding methods from machine learning, network science, and algorithms under a common evaluation protocol; a joint assessment of edge-level link prediction and graph-level topology reconstruction, which capture complementary aspects of embedding quality; a systematic investigation of how embedding performance varies with network structure across controlled synthetic networks and diverse empirical networks; and practical guidance on the structural regimes and evaluation tasks for which different embedding paradigms are most suitable.
