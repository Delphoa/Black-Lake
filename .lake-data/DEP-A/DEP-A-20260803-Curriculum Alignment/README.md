### `2606.19469-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: In the current research, we develop such a method and apply it longitudinally to an accredited Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, mapping the same program against both CS2013 and CS2023 through the three nested lenses of topical coverage, competency articulation, and cognitive depth. We therefore designed a staged, human-in-the-loop pipeline in which semantic retrieval proposes candidate matches, a human confirms them against an explicit definition of coverage, a second rater establishes the reliability of those judgments, and the confirmed map is then read for coverage, emphasis, accreditation-relevant gaps, competency articulation, cognitive depth, and longitudinal change. The program and the two guideline corpora feed a benchmarked retrieve-then-confirm pipeline, whose confirmed relations are read through three coverage lenses, namely knowledge, skill with cognitive depth, and disposition, and turned into the reported analyses; the same procedure is applied longitudinally to CS2013 and CS2023.
