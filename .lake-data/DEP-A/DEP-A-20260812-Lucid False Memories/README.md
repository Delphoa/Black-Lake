### `2607.15657-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: Separately, transfer-based adversarial attacks on vision encoders [ 88 , 33 , 20 , 82 , 48 , 39 , 76 ] have been studied in single-turn classification and retrieval settings but have not been applied to the persistent memory pipeline of multimodal AI agents. We introduce Lucid , a black-box adversarial pipeline for corrupting the persistent multimodal long-term memory of AI agents in three stages. We propose Lucid , a black-box adversarial framework that compromises multimodal memory pipelines under a strictly image-bounded threat model , requiring no access to the target MLLM, target retrieval encoder, or the text channel.
