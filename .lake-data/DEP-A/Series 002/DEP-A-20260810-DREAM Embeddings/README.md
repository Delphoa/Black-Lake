Its central technical focus is: The strong performance of DREAM-8B suggests that the proposed training paradigm scales favorably and continues to produce competitive retrieval models. While these methods demonstrate the potential of next-token prediction for retrieval, it remains unclear how to fully exploit autoregressive language modeling as a supervision signal for training dense retrievers. In this work, we propose DREAM, a method that connects a dense retriever to a frozen LLM through attention, enabling next-token prediction loss to directly supervise retrieval training.
