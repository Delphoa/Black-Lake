Although oversampling techniques such as Synthetic Minority Oversampling Technique (SMOTE) and Adaptive Synthetic Sampling (ADASYN) are widely adopted to mitigate class imbalance, their effectiveness for hierarchical CWE text classification remains largely unexplored. Evaluated on a CWE Research Concept dataset, the proposed model achieves a weighted F1-score of 0.76 0.76 without data augmentation, outperforming all baselines with notable gains on minority classes, including the Class category whose F1-score improves from 0.49 0.49 to 0.60 0.60 over the BERT baseline.
