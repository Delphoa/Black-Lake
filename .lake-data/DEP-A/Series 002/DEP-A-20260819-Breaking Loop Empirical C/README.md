Its central technical focus is: Industrial recommenders typically address cold start with content-based features in the candidate generator and ranker; deep models trained on user-item engagement, including the YouTube architecture in ( 9 ) and its multi-task successor in ( 29 ) , have largely absorbed cold-start handling into the feature pipeline. ( 27 ) proposed a listwise cross-entropy framework to estimate position bias without randomized traffic. Our contribution complements this line of work with online evidence on a production music surface, and a head-to-head comparison of interventions that take different views of where the loop should be broken.
