Its central technical focus is: As shown in Figure 2 , UrbanAgent is a two-stage framework that integrates tool-augmented agentic learning with multi-agent collaborative reasoning for urban region profiling. The results show that UrbanAgent consistently outperforms existing DL and multi-agent baselines by 8.1% on the R 2 R^{2} metric, validating the effectiveness of collaborative reasoning and evidence-driven inference for urban region profiling. In this work, we propose a structured reasoning framework that explicitly preserves and reconciles heterogeneous urban modalities, enabling verifiable and interpretable urban region profiling.
