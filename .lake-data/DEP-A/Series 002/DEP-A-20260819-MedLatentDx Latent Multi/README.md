Its central technical focus is: To address these two challenges, we introduce MedLatentDx , a latent multi-agent communication framework for cross-hospital rare-disease diagnosis. We formulate cross-hospital rare-disease diagnosis as a clinical-text-restricted latent communication problem, where hospital agents exchange compact latent blocks rather than retrieved clinical text during inference. We evaluate both diagnostic utility and empirical clinical-content leakage, showing that MedLatentDx improves cross-hospital diagnostic performance while reducing reconstructable clinical content relative to other latent communication baselines.
