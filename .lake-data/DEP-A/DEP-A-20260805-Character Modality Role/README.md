### `2605.09443-whitepaper-review.md`

Reconstructs the paper's mechanism and formal objective, audits the evaluation and reported results, separates paper report from reviewer inference, and records complete section, table, figure, equation, appendix, disclosure, and reproducibility coverage. Its central technical focus is: Recent studies further enhance persona consistency through memory retrieval, structured knowledge, and explicit cognitive simulation [ park2023generativeagents , Sun2024IdentityDrivenHR , xu2024characterdestiny , park2024charactergpt , wang2025rolerag , wang2025coser , tang2025thinking , tang2026character ] . Our contributions are summarized as follows: (1) We identify Modality-Role Interference (MRI) in Multimodal RPAs, highlighting the cognitive mismatch between objective MLLM visual features and subjective character perception, and propose the Through the Lens of Character paradigm; (2) We introduce CAVI, an training-free framework that systematically resolves MRI via macro-level token pruning (CTP), micro-level orthogonal feature modulation (OFM), and decoding-level modality-adaptive steering (MARS); (3) Supported by information-theoretic analysis, extensive experiments indicate that CAVI effectively alleviates MRI, significantly enhancing the overall performance of Multimodal RPAs. To address this, we introduce the training-free Character-Aware Visual Intervention (CAVI) framework, enabling agents to perceive the world through the lens of character.
