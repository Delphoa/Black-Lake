Its central technical focus is: Guided by this vision, we design and build TensorCast , a distributed tensor management layer for LLM infrastructure. Our results demonstrate that TensorCast achieves competitive performance with specialized tensor management systems in model weight materialization (§ 6.1 ), weight synchronization (§ 6.2 ), and KV cache management (§ 6.3 ), while enabling new cross-component optimization policies through programmable lifecycle composition. We identify tensor lifecycle management as a missing abstraction layer in modern LLM infrastructure and characterize the common lifecycle primitives shared across diverse tensor management workloads.
