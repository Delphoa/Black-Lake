## Insights and Relevance

The manuscript connects partial-observation sequence modeling with physical control design. Its central engineering lesson is that better temporal memory, cheaper training curricula, and greater actuator authority are coupled levers: quarter-chord pitching exposes an acceleration-dependent added-mass contribution that changes what the policy can control. The manuscript is the durable research artifact for this run; the operational `.logs` note and detailed `.reports` Report-Mark preserve the selection, validation, related-entry synthesis, and review challenges that led to it.
