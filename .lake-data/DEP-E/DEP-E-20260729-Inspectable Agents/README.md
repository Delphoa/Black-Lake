## Summary

This DEP-E reviews ten research findings collected by the source DEP as a connected evidence set for evaluating and governing autonomous agents. Its central synthesis is that reliable systems need inspectable observation, explicit decision records, bounded execution authority, and outcome receipts. It distinguishes source claims from reviewer interpretation and records the limits of organization-authored reports, abstract-only evidence, proprietary model access, and unreplicated experiments.

The new expansion in this pass examines the full J-space/global-workspace paper, Anthropic's official summary, and the official `jacobian-lens` reference implementation. The paper reports causal evidence that a sparse, vocabulary-aligned subspace participates in verbal report and flexible reasoning, while also showing that automatic cognition can bypass that space. The artifact therefore treats internal readouts as an additional observation surface—not as a substitute for behavioral tests, access controls, provenance, or human authority.

## Relevance

- Joins interpretability signals to operational receipts rather than treating internal probes as standalone assurance.
- Relates benchmark integrity, belief-action gaps, modular capability control, governed memory, and physical interlocks.
- Preserves negative evidence and unresolved limitations needed by the next reviewer.
- Proposes three bounded exercises and an MVP for evidence-linked agent evaluation.
