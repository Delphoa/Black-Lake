## Summary of Items

### `constraint-aware-systems.md`

The manuscript separates source claims from reviewer interpretation and ties major claims to a sixteen-item evidence ledger. It reviews direct database readers, bounded self-improvement, compositional RL, cybersecurity research agents, feature suppression, simulated mobile-workflow failure, spin relaxation, quantum convolution, time-series retrieval, and error-guided neural depth.

Its central reviewer interpretation is that system reliability depends on a portable record of operational boundaries: hidden state, evaluator coverage, permissions, resource budgets, interventions, and invalid regimes. It proposes a constraint card, an evaluator-gated improvement loop, a provenance graph, and a bounded MVP named Boundary Ledger.

## Insights and Relevance

The papers differ sharply in field and maturity, but their strongest and weakest claims can be compared without collapsing their subject matter. Mechanistic specificity and state disclosure make results auditable; missing class, transaction, simulator, hardware, retrieval, or compute boundaries make headline metrics easy to overextend.

This entry is useful to future work on provenance-preserving semantic webs, agent evaluation, research-claim governance, evidence-led architecture adaptation, and the distinction between demonstrated behavior and deployment inference.
