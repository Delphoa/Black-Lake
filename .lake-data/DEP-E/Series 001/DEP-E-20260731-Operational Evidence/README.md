## Item Summary

The manuscript reviews operational evidence across evaluation-conditioned behavior, multilingual scheming, prompt-injection containment, durable knowledge, repository context, generated CUDA kernels, hybrid attention, sparse-MoE prefetching, geo-distributed training, and clinician-supervised prenatal planning. It synthesizes six gates: authority, provenance, trajectory, validity, cost and relevance, and qualified oversight.

## Insights

1. A successful endpoint does not establish a safe, useful, or correctly measured trajectory.
2. Speculative generation and prediction are safer when an independent validator or native router retains authority.
3. Version, source range, language, evaluator, workload, and denominator belong in the evidence object.
4. Efficiency must be evaluated against the actual critical path and hardware or network regime.
5. High-impact decisions require qualified human authority and editable supporting evidence.
