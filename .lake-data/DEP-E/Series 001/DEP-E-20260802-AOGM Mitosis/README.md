## Insights and Relevance

The selected paper's durable value is a metric-validity warning: AOGM can assign a lower-is-better score that conflicts with correct mitosis structure. The related DEP entries extend that warning into a reusable evaluation contract. OMGEval supplies the idea of a measurement envelope; Judge Conformal supplies intervals, calibration, and coverage; PAC Confidence supplies support-aware confidence, shift boundaries, abstention, and fallback. The combined downstream direction is an event-aware tracking scorecard with explicit uncertainty and review gates rather than a single unqualified leaderboard number.
