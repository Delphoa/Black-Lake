## Executive Summary

The ten findings support a reviewer hypothesis: reliability improves when systems make their operating boundary explicit and attach evidence to the transition from model output to action, claim, or resource allocation. GKR-HND and the neutral-atom roadmap make verification part of the computation contract; ARA and MisKnow-Agent show why executable code or document-level verification is insufficient when assumptions and evidence can fail inside a workflow; and the memory, SciExplore, and CAPTAIN papers move context delivery and signal history into the system boundary.

The medical and systems papers show the same idea under different constraints. Multimodal EEG reports strong benchmark performance but a much lower patient-independent LOSO balanced accuracy; agent-guided surgical concepts improve reported metrics but remain preclinical; and Faster IndexTTS-2 demonstrates that latency, streaming, batching, and quality must be measured together. Evidence is strong for the reported methods and metrics, but this artifact does not claim independent reproduction, production readiness, clinical utility, security certification, or quantum advantage.

## Detailed Summary

### Verification and validity boundaries

GKR-HND proposes a registered-model protocol for Homomorphic–Nonhomomorphic Decomposition Transformers. The retained verifier checks the GKR transcript and registered-weight openings while a computation worker handles expensive public evaluations. The source claim is conditional on an honest retained verifier and prover-worker non-collusion; the evidence establishes a proof path in pretrained HND models, not a general serving guarantee.

ARA translates natural-language questions into structured causal protocols, generates synthetic data from Structural Causal Models with known effects, and tests controlled violations of identification assumptions. The authors report that protocol construction and adversarial validation did not consistently improve numerical agreement against standard LLM generation. Their reported benefit is a change in failure mode: protocol concerns, diagnostic failures, incomplete inference, or downgraded non-causal interpretations can replace silently returned causal estimates.

MisKnow-Agent constructs 5,933 quality-controlled misleading documents for DeepResearch Bench tasks and measures report-level false-conclusion adoption. The source reports that one misleading document raises mean FCAR from 0% in the no-injection control to 54.7% across tested configurations. Cross-model verification can classify the documents as misleading, yet workflow agents may still adopt the corresponding false conclusion. Verification must persist while evidence enters intermediate research states, not only at a final document check.

### Delivery, context, and evidence grounding

The cue-anchored memory paper distinguishes durable documents from situational working memory and proposes path, symbol, semantic, event, and temporal triggers evaluated by the harness. In the reported coding task, voluntary memory use was zero in 114 turns despite a pre-seeded store; deterministic injection delivered seeded memories with zero false alarms; and repeated compaction removed conversation-only facts in 106 of 108 forced compactions while harness delivery preserved them through 138 compact-resumes. These are mechanism signals for delivery-first memory, not a general production evaluation.

SciExplore uses 103 expert-curated tasks across more than ten disciplines, covering scientific database navigation, ambiguous literature retrieval, missing-reference completion, and cross-source structured synthesis. The source reports substantial performance degradation as task complexity rises and extremely low accuracy on the hardest evidence-grounded synthesis tasks. Search trace length is not a substitute for evidence-level correctness.

CAPTAIN encodes recent log history, bridges compact context into a decoder, scores entries with perplexity, and smooths the time series. Its source claim is that this can compete with strong APT-detection baselines under substantially less curated inputs. Deployment still requires environment-shift, alert-cost, privacy, and analyst-workflow evaluation.

### Shift and human-consequence boundaries

The EEG source combines a Mamba raw-signal encoder, a time-frequency ViT-style encoder, and a lightweight text encoder in a shared space. On CHB-MIT, it reports 0.874 AUROC for the best single model and 0.878 for an ensemble. Mean LOSO balanced accuracy of 0.558 across 19 subjects is the more consequential boundary signal: a favorable standard split does not establish patient-independent performance. This artifact treats the paper as non-diagnostic research evidence.

The surgical-margin paper uses agent-guided concept discovery over REIMS data, refines semantic descriptions and diagnostic weights, and grounds concepts in a biochemical knowledge graph. The source reports an approximate 7% relative improvement in skin balanced accuracy, breast balanced accuracy of 0.87, and fewer false positives in a representative intraoperative case. The record marks the paper accepted to MICCAI 2026, but the inspected document is the submission version. Prospective clinical evaluation remains outside this run.

### Resource and execution boundaries

Faster IndexTTS-2 accelerates the GPT, flow-matching DiT, and vocoder stages with TensorRT and TensorRT-LLM, adding streaming and batching. On a single NVIDIA A100 80GB setup, the paper reports 3.60x end-to-end speedup on English and 3.46x on Chinese relative to the PyTorch baseline, with GPT speedups of about 5.0x and 4.8x. It evaluates 1,088 English and 2,020 Chinese samples and reports WER, speaker similarity, naturalness, latency, real-time factor, time-to-first-audio, and throughput. Smaller streaming chunks reduce first-audio latency but increase total work and can degrade naturalness.

The neutral-atom strategic plan defines practical quantum advantage using correctness, advantage over available classical hardware, scaling advantage, and relevance outside the builders. It connects qubit-array scaling, encodings, below-threshold logical performance, continuous reloading, fast readout, photonic control, error correction, compilation, and networking. The plan is a systems roadmap, not evidence that practical quantum advantage has already been achieved.

### Cross-cutting synthesis

Across the package, a system becomes more trustworthy when the boundary is a measured interface rather than an implicit assumption. Verification must bind computation to the intended model; memory must bind a fact to the situation that needs it; research agents must bind conclusions to checked evidence; medical models must bind benchmark results to patient and operating-condition shifts; serving systems must bind throughput to quality and latency; and quantum claims must bind advantage to correctness, classical comparison, scaling, and external utility.

## Observations

- Observed pattern: The sources relocate reliability from the model alone to a harness, protocol, verifier, benchmark, scheduler, or hardware-software contract.
- Observed pattern: Evidence integrity fails inside workflows. MisKnow-Agent shows that a source can be recognized as misleading yet still influence a final conclusion; ARA shows that executable code can still encode invalid causal assumptions.
- Technical implication: A review system should record what evidence was retrieved, when it entered working state, how it was checked, and which claim or action consumed it.
- Technical implication: Benchmark splits should include compaction, patient holdouts, operating-room shift, minimally curated logs, hardware variation, and classical comparison.
- Contradiction or tension: Strong source results often come from narrow controlled settings while practical value is framed broadly. That is a transfer hypothesis, not deployment evidence.
- Open question: Can one provenance and boundary schema support agents, clinical models, cyber detection, GPU serving, and quantum experiments without becoming too generic?
- Reviewer hypothesis: Evidence-bearing state transitions may be a more stable unit for reliability engineering than model capability labels alone.

## Considerations

Adoption requires a clear owner for evidence, a versioned source manifest, and a decision boundary that says what happens when verification fails. Systems should minimize sensitive data, preserve access controls, record uncertainty, support abstention, and make rollback or human review routine. For medical and security work, outputs should remain advisory until prospective or authorized evaluation establishes safety. For serving and datacenter systems, quality degradation, queueing, cost, thermal behavior, and dependency drift must be monitored together. For quantum work, claims should be compared against current classical methods and accompanied by an explicit verification story.

A heterogeneous synthesis can overfit to a pleasing theme. The boundary hypothesis is a reviewer interpretation and should be tested against counterexamples.

## Potential Implementations

- Evidence-bound research workspace: Reviewers use a repository-relative source manifest, evidence IDs, claim map, and reviewer ledger. The system stores public-safe locators and derived notes, emits an auditable manuscript, and requires human review before publication.
- Harness-owned context gate: Agent infrastructure injects bounded, provenance-labeled context at deterministic lifecycle events, records trigger evaluations, checks staleness, and falls back to voluntary lookup only when the gate is not decisive.
- Validity-first science runner: A research agent produces a structured protocol, synthetic validation plan, diagnostics, and an abstaining output before it can consume governed data.
- Defensive evidence-integrity benchmark: An authorized benchmark inserts labeled synthetic misleading documents into a closed corpus and measures report-level adoption, verification timing, and correction quality.
- Shift-aware medical evaluation harness: Synthetic or authorized de-identified signals are used to compare standard splits with patient, device, and operating-condition holdouts; outputs remain non-diagnostic.
- Context-augmented defensive log triage: In an authorized lab, score synthetic or permitted logs with temporal context and smoothing, then measure analyst workload, false-positive cost, privacy exposure, and environment transfer.
- Resource-aware serving gate: Compare optimized speech or agent-serving configurations under fixed quality thresholds, time-to-first-output, throughput, queueing, and cost budgets.
- Quantum verification notebook: Use simulators or public benchmark data to compare a candidate quantum task with classical baselines, define correctness and resource metrics, and record which advantage criteria remain unproven.

## Example MVP Product

- Product name: Evidence Boundary Gate.

- Problem: Source-grounded findings often lose provenance when a model output becomes a claim, action, or resource decision.
