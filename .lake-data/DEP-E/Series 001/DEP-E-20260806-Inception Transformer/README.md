## Insights and Relevance

iFormer is relevant to compact vision-backbone design because it treats local detail and global context as complementary feature-routing responsibilities. Its relationship to AFIDAF’s spectral/image-domain mixing, SSP’s spatially structured detection supervision, and HeightFormer’s localized transformer attention suggests a shared evaluation question: whether structured routing improves the accuracy/compute trade-off after controlling for training recipe, task head, uncertainty, and real device cost. The artifact is intended for research planning and evidence-aware implementation, not deployment approval.
