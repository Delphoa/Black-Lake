## Insights and Relevance

The manuscript's durable insight is that a compact contact representation can be expanded into a fuller overlap representation when each expansion is bounded and the reduction predicate preserves infinite-loop reachability. This creates a useful bridge to three existing Black Lake artifacts: Moran Spectra contributes scale-indexed fractal constructions and invariant control; Flag Hardy Operators contributes geometry-aware multiscale proof decomposition; and Acoustic Phase Retrieval contributes observability and conditioning gates before reconstruction. The operational log records selection and source-integrity gates, while the Report-Mark records the detailed synthesis and exactly three implementation bridges.

The DEP is research-class rather than a frozen theorem record because the construction invites independent implementation, broader graph-directed generalization, and complexity measurement. The paper's proof claims are preserved as source claims; no finite implementation run is presented as proof.
