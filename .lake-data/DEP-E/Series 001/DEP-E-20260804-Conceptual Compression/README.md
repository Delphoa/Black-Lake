## Insights and Relevance

The paper makes compression an interface between representation and downstream use: sparse structure supports layout and analysis, while a learned texture representation enables perceptual reconstruction and editing. The related rate-distortion, context-codec, and semantic-head records show how to extend that idea with explicit resource vectors, verifiable commitments, and controller-guided allocation. The manuscript is the durable research artifact; the matching `.logs` entry records selection and operational validation, while the `.reports` Report-Mark records the detailed synthesis and exact-three implementation/code contracts.
