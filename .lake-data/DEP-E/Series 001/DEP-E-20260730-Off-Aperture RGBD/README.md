## Insights and Relevance

The paper's key reusable idea is to treat DOE placement as an optical information-allocation decision: an off-aperture position can offer more local control of wide-angle aberrations than a conventional aperture-plane placement. The research is relevant to compact RGBD cameras, computational imaging, and calibrated 3D perception, but its source-reported gains should be interpreted alongside compute limits, fabrication artifacts, and the need for independent geometric validation.
