## Summary of Items

`README.md` defines the deposit boundary and records that scholarly source documents were withheld locally. It supplies every public source locator used by the deposit and makes the medical-safety boundary explicit.

`dental_lung_mr_manuscript.md` reviews the paper's two-step, two-sample Mendelian-randomization design. It preserves the reported dental-caries associations with overall and subtype lung cancer, the small FVC/FEV1 mediation estimates, the null periodontitis result, and the diagnostics that constrain interpretation. It also records an abstract-versus-Table-I exposure-sample discrepancy, significant heterogeneity, directional-pleiotropy signals in part of the mediation chain, European-only ancestry, unaddressed multiplicity, and the absence of independent reproduction.

## Insights and Relevance

The durable value is an evidence contract for biomedical causal claims. A point estimate must travel with its phenotype definition, exposure scale, instrument set, ancestry, estimator, heterogeneity and pleiotropy diagnostics, multiplicity policy, source version, and intervention boundary. The three related DEP entries extend that contract into causal-workload preservation, governed GWAS execution, and externally validated clinical prediction. Together they show why a research association should pass explicit statistical, governance, domain-shift, and human-review gates before becoming a clinical decision surface.
