## Summary of Items

The paper introduces PFGM++, a family that embeds data in an additional `D` dimensions and uses `D` to interpolate between PFGM (`D=1`) and diffusion-model behavior (`D` approaching infinity). The inspected CIFAR-10 table reports unconditional FID 1.91 for `D=2048` and class-conditional FID 1.74, while the FFHQ table reports a best minimum FID of 2.43 for `D=128`. These measurements support the paper's robustness-versus-rigidity argument but remain author-reported until independently reproduced.
