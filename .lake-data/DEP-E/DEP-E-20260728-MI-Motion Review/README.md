## Insights and Relevance

MI-Motion separates local pose, globally aligned pose, and root-trajectory error while testing scene-dependent interactions. Its SocialTGCN results are competitive but mixed across scenes and metrics, making it more useful as an evaluation substrate than as a universal method claim. The synthesis connects interactive 3D generation, sequence-based pose representations, and trajectory-safety evaluation without treating them as interchangeable.
