The manuscript separates source claims from reviewer interpretation and traces major claims through an evidence ledger. ## Insights and Relevance

The agent-specific works form a practical stack. Versioned knowledge and skills supply attributable context; canonical resource resolution protects intake; typed tool contracts and least privilege constrain action; causal trace slices support debugging; deployment-rule interventions expose institutional effects; and effect-based severity measures what escaped. The scientific-reasoning and quantum papers broaden the concept without being treated as agent-safety evidence.

This makes the entry useful for future research on composable agent control, benchmark governance, provenance-preserving semantic webs, and defensive tool-use architectures.
