## Insights and Relevance

The paper links distributed computation over wireless superposition to OFDM resource structure: function decomposition controls who participates, sub-carrier allocation controls where computation occurs, and power allocation controls the weakest-node bottleneck. It is relevant to AirComp, domain-aware wireless processing, and interference-constrained spectrum allocation, while its simulation-only evidence and idealized assumptions make reproduction and hardware-in-the-loop validation necessary before deployment decisions.
