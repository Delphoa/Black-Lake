## Insights and Relevance

This entry studies LALIC as a design pattern for combining global linear-complexity context, local two-dimensional mixing, and causal entropy modeling in learned image compression. The synthesis connects LALIC with a state-space image-compression review, a structure/texture conceptual codec, and an efficient local/global visual-mixing review. The manuscript is the durable research record; the operational selection and validation trail is preserved in `.logs/20260819-Arxiv-LALIC-LOG.md`, while the detailed review and synthesis are preserved in `.reports/BL-Arxiv-LALIC-20260819/Report-Mark.md`.

The entry is useful for future codec comparisons, reproducibility planning, and edge-deployment design. It does not claim that the reported results were independently reproduced, that theoretical linear complexity equals end-to-end efficiency, or that generative or structured compression is lossless.
