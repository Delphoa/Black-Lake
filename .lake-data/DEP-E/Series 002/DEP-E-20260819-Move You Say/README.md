# DEP-E-20260819-Move You Say

Tags: arXiv, human motion generation, scene affordance, 3D grounding, diffusion

This public-safe DEP-E deposit reviews language-guided human motion generation with an explicit scene-affordance representation. It records source-grounded findings, implementation implications, related Black Lake research, and validation status. Original source files and derived caches were withheld locally.

## Contents

- README.md — public-safe deposit overview and attribution.

## Summary of Items

The manuscript covers the paper's problem, two-stage Affordance Map Diffusion Model and Affordance-aware Motion Diffusion Model, datasets, reported metrics, limitations, evidence ledger, implementation considerations, and three ways to exercise the research.

## Insights and Relevance

The main reusable idea is an explicit spatial-temporal affordance contract between language, scene geometry, and human motion. It offers a practical seam for diagnostics and human correction. The related DEP entries broaden the comparison across controllable motion, realistic scene coverage, and language-conditioned 3D layout reasoning.
